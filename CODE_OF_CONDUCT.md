# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable.

## Expected Behavior

- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Focus discussions on the work and the people who use it.
- Respect different experiences and levels of expertise.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and any conduct that would make a reasonable contributor feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository owner, **Kermighty**, through the contact method listed on the project's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and addressed fairly.

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
